About Me

Who am I?

I'm Senne Lenaerts and my love for gaming started at a young age when I got a wii when I was a child. Before switching to playstation in my teens and later to PC gaming. My favourite game franchises are the Souls Series by FromSoftware and the Project Moon Games. Video games have thaugt me a lot, and opened up the way to my love for worldbuilding and storytelling. Pushing me to try my hand at bulding my own worlds and stories.

Things I enjoy

Listening to music

If I could music would be playing in my ears 24/7. Musicians I Like: English The Smiths, The Arctic Monkeys, and Radiohead. Japanese King Gnu, FAKE TYPE, Syudou, and Tyrkouaz.

Reading & writing

My current fascination is reading literary classics like: Metamorphosis, The Stranger, or Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?. In my free time I also enjoy writing my own stories, and have a project in the works.

Playing & creating games

Game has become less of a daily activity I partake in, but something I still enjoy doing from time to time. They are great source of inspiration after all. Now I mostly try to focus on putting ideas into pratice making my own expieriences that will hopefully convey the same feelings and emotions I felt when playing games that inspired me.
