A little about me

I'm Senne Lenaerts. My love for gaming started when I was a child across multiple platforms. My favourite game series include the Souls series by FromSoftware and the games by Project Moon. My inspirations are Yoko Taro for his unique storytelling, and hidetaka Miyazaki for his worldbuilding and game design. I aspire to create games that are as impactful and memorable as the ones that inspired me.

Many forms of media have made me into the person I am today. Bellow this section you will get to know me even better through my tastes, passions and hobbies.
