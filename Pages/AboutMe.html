A little about me

I'm Senne Lenaerts. My love for gaming started when I was a child. My favourite game series include the Soulsborn series by FromSoftware, NieR Series and the games by Project Moon. My inspirations for games are Yoko Taro for his unique storytelling, and hidetaka Miyazaki for his worldbuilding and game design. I aspire to create games that are as impactful and memorable as the ones that inspired me.

Where The Path Leads

Currently Writing

Where The Path Leads is a book series I am writing. Its about a man named Samuel having to navigate his way though a world set on a course for destruction. The first book takes place in Pilantrop, the City of Inovation. Where he is a refugie under the care of a company of clockworkers. In the first book he is mostly a spectator, onlu due to happenings transpiring within the city does he act, but as the series goes on he is forced to make difficult choices and question where himself entirely. So that he may someday find his brighter future.
